1Introduction

The Axon Admin Dashboard is the web console for the Axon conversational banking assistant. It gives your team one place to see how customers use the assistant, how well it performs, and to manage the institutions, languages, messages and settings behind it.

Monitor

Track audience growth, conversation volume, banking journeys, AI quality, response speed and system activity, each compared with the previous period.

Manage

Maintain institutions and licenses, dashboard users, assistant settings, reference data, message templates, conversation flows, languages and FAQs.

Who this guide is for

Dashboard accounts have one of three roles: Super Admin, Manager or Viewer. This guide is written for all three. Viewers will mostly use the Dashboard and Analytics sections; administrators also use the management modules in chapter 5.

Where the data comes from

Every figure on screen is loaded live from the Axon Admin API. The dashboard never calculates comparisons itself: the API supplies each value, its previous value, the percentage change and the explanation behind it. Refresh a page at any time to load the latest figures.

2Getting started

2.1 Signing in

Open the dashboard address provided by your administrator and sign in with your username and password. If you have forgotten your password, use Forgot password on the sign-in page; a single-use reset link lets you choose a new one. Sessions expire after a period of inactivity, in which case you are returned to the sign-in page.

2.2 Finding your way around

The left sidebar groups every page. Use the round arrow on its edge to collapse or expand it.

GroupPages
OverviewDashboard, Analytics, Institutions, Logs
AdministrationDashboard Users, Configurations, Master Data, Messages, Flows, Languages, FAQs
AccountUser Profile

The top bar contains the breadcrumb trail, a global search (press Ctrl + K from anywhere), the light/dark theme switch and your account menu. Each page also shows when it was Last Updated with a blue refresh button beside it.

2.3 Light, dark and mobile

The dashboard follows your preference for light or dark appearance and can be switched at any time with the moon icon. It is fully responsive: on a phone the sidebar becomes a slide-out menu and cards stack in a single column.

4.1 Reporting period

The period selector at the top right of the page controls every card on the tab. Choose a quick range such as Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days or Last 30 days, or pick a custom start and end date. Many cards also carry their own period button so you can compare windows side by side. While a panel is open the page behind it is softly blurred to keep your focus on the choice.

4.2 Reading a metric card

Every headline card follows the same pattern: the figure, a coloured change badge, and the comparison it refers to (for example vs last week).

BadgeMeaning
▲ +13.6% greenA favourable change. For measures where lower is better, such as abandonment or latency, a decrease is shown in green.
▼ −21.4% redAn unfavourable change compared with the previous period.
0% greyNo change, or a figure that is shown for context only.

The ⓘ information button

Select or hover over the ⓘ beside a card title for a plain-language explanation: what the number means, the formula behind it and a worked example. The page dims and blurs while the explanation is open. Click the button to keep it open, and press Esc or click elsewhere to close it.

4.3Audience

Understand your customer base and whether people return.

  1. Tab bar: switch between Audience, Conversations, Journeys, AI Quality, Performance and Activity Logs.
  2. Reporting period: the window every card on the tab reports on.
  3. Headline cards: Total Unique Users, Total Sessions, Daily Active Users and Current Active Sessions.
  4. ⓘ button: what the figure means, its formula and an example.
  5. Active Audience: a daily activity curve with summary cards for today and the selected period.
  6. Language Preferences: share of customers by preferred language, number of languages and total users.
  7. Audience & Retention: retention, returning and one-time users, sessions per user, daily-to-monthly activity, and users inactive for 30+ days.
  8. New User Acquisition: new users today, this week and this month, share of traffic, growth rates and single-session drop rate.

4.4Conversations

See how much customers talk to the assistant and when they are busiest.

  • Headline cards: Total Messages, Total Sessions, Session Engagement and Turns Per Conversation.
  • Activity: one card with a switch between Activity By Hour (to find peak hours) and Activity By Weekday (to find the busiest days).
TIP

Use the hourly view to plan support cover and maintenance windows: schedule changes in the quietest hours.

4.5Journeys

A journey is a multi-step banking task such as a fund transfer, bill payment or adding a beneficiary. This tab shows how many start, finish or are abandoned.

  • Headline cards: Journeys Started, Journeys Completed, Completion Rate and Abandonment Rate.
  • Journey Trends: started, completed and abandoned journeys over time.
  • Journeys By Category: which banking tasks customers start most. View all lists every category.
  • Completion Vs Abandonment Rate: for each category, the share finished against the share left unfinished. Categories with low completion are the best candidates for improvement.
  • Journey Outcomes Detail: a searchable table with total, completed, cancelled, failed, abandoned, completion rate and average steps per journey.

4.6AI Quality

Check that the assistant understands customers and keeps conversations safe.

  • Headline cards: Classified Interactions, Average Confidence, High-Confidence Rate and AI Fallback Rate.
  • Confidence Distribution: the share of requests understood with high, medium or low confidence. A large low-confidence slice means requests are being misread.
  • Safety Blocks Trend: how often safety checks blocked or flagged a message over time.
  • What Users Ask For: the topics customers raise most often. View all lists every one.

A card reading No Data means nothing was recorded for the selected period. It does not indicate a fault.

4.7Performance

Measure how quickly the assistant responds. For all speed measures, lower is better.

  • Headline cards: Measured Interactions, Average Response Time, 95th Percentile and Slow Responses (over 1,500 ms).
  • Average Time By Stage: where time is spent: parsing, intent classification, flow execution and AI generation.
  • Response Time Trend and Response Time Distribution: how speed changes over time and how responses spread across fast and slow ranges.
  • Slowest Interactions: the individual requests that took longest, for troubleshooting.
PERCENTILES EXPLAINED

The average hides slow outliers. P50 is the typical response; P95 is the time that 95% of responses beat. A large gap between them means some customers wait much longer than most.

4.8Activity Logs

Review how interactions ended and where errors occur.

  • Headline cards: Successful Interactions, Failed Interactions, Tokens Used and Recorded Errors.
  • Interaction Outcomes: a gauge of the success rate with successful and failed counts, beside Conversation Channels (voice versus text).
  • Speech Recognition Events and Token Usage: how voice input was transcribed, and how many tokens were spent on prompts and completions.
  • Actions Delivered: what the assistant did for customers, ranked by count.
  • Slowest Interactions and Recorded Errors: the slow requests, and errors over time with a breakdown by type.

5Administration modules

In this chapterInstitutions, Logs, Dashboard Users, Configurations, Master Data, Messages, Flows, Languages, FAQs and User Profile. Step-by-step tasks follow in chapter 6.

The Administration group and the Institutions and Logs pages let authorised users manage the assistant. Most list pages share the same controls: a search box, filters, a Rows per page selector, page navigation and a primary action button at the top right.

5.1 Institutions

Register financial institutions, issue license keys and track subscription expiry. Summary cards show total, active, expiring-soon (within 30 days) and expired licenses. Use New Institution to add one; each row offers actions such as editing and renewing the license.

5.2 Logs

A live view of assistant activity. The session list on the left filters the stream to one conversation. Real-Time streams events as they happen; History searches past events. Use Pause, Clear, Copy and Download to work with the stream, and Full screen for more room.

5.3 Dashboard Users

Create and manage the accounts that can sign in to this dashboard. Cards count Super Admins, Managers and Viewers. Search by name, email or role, filter by role, and use New User to add an account.

5.4 Configurations

Adjust how the assistant behaves. Settings are numeric or on/off and can be filtered by type. Changes apply as soon as they are saved, so edit with care.

5.5 Master Data

Maintain reference data in six tabs: BanksBillersDonationsCardsBiller TypesBiller Categories Each tab lists its records with an active flag and offers add, edit and delete.

5.6 Messages

Review and customise the automated voice and chat message templates across all supported languages. Each row shows the message key, its parameters, language, text and status.

5.7 Flows

Enable or disable conversational banking task flows. Each row is an intent with an Enabled switch; a disabled flow is no longer offered to customers.

5.8 Languages

Configure the languages the assistant supports and choose the primary language used by default. Add a language with New Language, and enable or disable existing ones.

5.9 FAQs

Keep customer support content up to date. Set the FAQ page header and introduction, organise questions into sections, and use Add FAQ to write a new question and answer. Filter by status to see drafts or published items.

5.10 User Profile

View your administrator identity, active credentials and assigned dashboard permissions. Open it from the sidebar or from your account menu.

6How do I…?

Short, step-by-step answers to the tasks administrators do most. Nothing is saved until you select the final button, and you can close any dialog with the × or Esc.

Before you startYou need an account with permission to manage the area. If a button is missing, ask a Super Admin to review your role.

6.1 Add an institution

  1. Open Institutions from the sidebar.
  2. Choose New Institution at the top right of Manage Institutions.
  3. Enter the Institution Name and pick the License Expires At date and time.
  4. Select Create Institution.
GOOD TO KNOW

The institution appears in the list with its license expiry and status. Use the icons in its Actions column to edit it or manage its license later.

6.2 Add a dashboard user

  1. Open Dashboard Users.
  2. Choose New User.
  3. Enter the Username and Email, and choose the Role.
  4. Select Create User.
GOOD TO KNOW

Roles control what the person can do: Super Admin, Manager or Viewer. Choose the lowest role that fits their job.

6.3 Add a language

  1. Open Languages.
  2. Choose New Language and pick the language from the list.
  3. Leave Enabled on so customers can use it.
  4. Select Create Language.
GOOD TO KNOW

To make it the default, use Set as Primary on its row. Only one language is primary at a time.

6.4 Add an FAQ

  1. Open FAQs and choose Add FAQ.
  2. Type a Section title, or choose an existing section.
  3. Write the Question and the Answer.
  4. Optionally open Display order to place it, and keep Active on to show it on the customer FAQ page.
  5. Select Save FAQ.
GOOD TO KNOW

Write answers the way a customer would ask the question. Short, direct answers work best.

6.5 Add a bank (or other reference data)

  1. Open Master Data and choose the Banks tab.
  2. Choose Add Bank.
  3. Enter the Bank Name and Abbreviation, and keep Active on.
  4. Select Save.
GOOD TO KNOW

Billers, donations, card types, biller types and biller categories follow the same pattern on their own tabs.

6.6 Change a configuration setting

  1. Open Configurations.
  2. Choose Edit above the list. The values become editable.
  3. Change a Value. The allowed range is shown beneath the field.
  4. Select Save on that row, then Cancel editing when you are done.
GOOD TO KNOW

Changes apply as soon as they are saved, and they affect how the assistant behaves for every customer. Change one setting at a time and note the original value.

7.2 Glossary

TermMeaning
SessionOne conversation a customer starts with the assistant.
TurnOne question from the customer and the assistant's reply.
DAU / WAU / MAUDistinct users active in a day, a rolling 7 days and a rolling 30 days.
StickinessDAU divided by MAU: how often monthly users return on a given day.
RetentionThe share of users who came back for more than one session.
Journey / FlowA multi-step banking task, such as a fund transfer or bill payment.
AbandonmentA started journey that the customer left unfinished.
IntentWhat the customer is trying to do, as recognised by the assistant.
ConfidenceHow sure the assistant was when it recognised the intent.
FallbackA request handed to the generative AI model because no standard flow fitted.
LatencyThe time from the customer's message to the assistant's reply, in milliseconds.
TokenA unit of text processed by the language model; usage drives cost.

7.3 Troubleshooting

SymptomWhat to try
Cards show no data, or a loading errorCheck your connection, then refresh with the blue button. If you are returned to the sign-in page, your session has expired: sign in again.
A card reads No DataNothing was recorded in the selected period. Widen the period, for example to Last 30 days.
A figure differs from the previous pagePages may use different reporting periods. Check the period selector on each page.
An ⓘ shows general text onlyThe API sent no specific explanation for that card, so a built-in description is shown instead.
Live Logs show “Reconnecting…”The live connection is re-establishing. If it persists, refresh the page or contact your administrator.
A page or action is missingYour role may not include it. Ask a Super Admin to review your permissions.
